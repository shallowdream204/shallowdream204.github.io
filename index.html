Yuang Ai

Hi everyone, welcome to my website!

I'm Yuang Ai, a first-year PhD student at MMLab, the Chinese University of Hong Kong, supervised by Prof. Xiangyu Yue. I am also a research intern at ByteDance Seed, advised by Yi Jiang.

My recent research interests primarily focus on topics with significant real-world applications, like foundation generative models, unified multimodal models, etc.

I am open to any discussion or collaboration. If you are interested, please feel free to contact me via email.
